Our Story

Over 50 Years of Mystique

Krewe Mystique de la Capitale was founded in 1976 and stands as the oldest continuously parading krewe in Baton Rouge. For more than five decades, we have been at the heart of Mardi Gras celebrations, bringing tradition, pageantry, and community spirit to our city. Our Mission

At the core of Krewe Mystique de la Capitale is a commitment to creating meaningful memories for our members, guests, and the Baton Rouge community. We believe in preserving the joy, pageantry, and traditions of Mardi Gras while building friendships and connections that endure beyond parade season.
